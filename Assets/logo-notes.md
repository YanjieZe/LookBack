Strong simple silhouette, generous negative space, optically balanced geometry, rounded terminals, distinctive and calm. Flat solid near-black ink, genuinely transparent background. Centered square composition, mark occupies about 65% of canvas. Production logo asset, crisp clean vector-like edges. No words, no letters, no earbuds drawing, no arrows, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no multiple variants, no watermark. Understated refined contemporary identity, legible at small icon sizes.
